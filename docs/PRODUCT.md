# Product Name

Legacy Sky

---

# Vision

Every life leaves a light.

Legacy Sky helps accomplished adults preserve their stories, wisdom, values, and life lessons through thoughtful conversations, transforming memories into a lasting legacy for future generations.

We believe legacy is not measured by fame.

It is measured by the lives we touch.

A legacy is not truly preserved until it can be passed on.

---

# Mission

Help people feel:

- Heard
- Understood
- Remembered

And help them leave behind something meaningful that will outlive them.

Most successful people have accumulated:

- Stories
- Lessons
- Wisdom
- Regrets
- Values

Yet very little of it is preserved.

Their children often inherit:

- Photos
- Money
- Possessions

But not:

# Product Promise

We do not help users chat with AI.

We help users preserve their legacy.

## Interview Continuity

Legacy interviews may span multiple sessions.

Users can safely leave and return at any time.

Stories, drafts, and reflections are automatically preserved throughout the interview process.

Legacy Sky is designed to support long-form reflection rather than single-session conversations.

# AI Transparency

Legacy Sky uses AI to help users organize, summarize, and interpret their stories.

AI-generated content may include:

- Story summaries
- Lessons learned
- Value extraction
- Legacy portraits
- Letters

These outputs represent AI interpretations, not objective facts.

Users always have the final authority to:

- Review
- Edit
- Approve
- Delete

all generated content.

Nothing becomes part of a user's permanent legacy without their approval.

# Accessibility & Inclusivity

Legacy Sky is designed for adults reflecting on their life stories.

The experience should remain comfortable and accessible for users with varying levels of technical ability, vision, and language preferences.

Key principles:

- Large and readable typography
- High contrast visual design
- Voice-first interaction support
- Optional text-to-speech playback
- Keyboard accessibility
- Multi-language support

Accessibility should be considered a core product feature rather than an enhancement.
